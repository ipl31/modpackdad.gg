    About these results

    Overall rank, energy, and leaderboard position are reported by EVXL. Scenario best scores and rank thresholds come from the public KovaaK’s benchmark data used by EVXL. A scenario’s rank is the highest threshold its score reaches; “Unranked” means below Iron.

    The skill chart uses the strongest scenario in each skill and interpolates progress between its rank thresholds. This chart does not calculate the overall Voltaic rank. These are benchmark best scores, while the routine view tracks typical performance using daily medians.
